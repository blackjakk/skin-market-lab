SMLX-2 · Index & Settlement Methodology

Skin Market Lab · methodology version SMLX-2 · any rule change bumps the version — a fixing is meaningless without its rulebook. This document describes a measurement, not an offer, solicitation, or operation of any financial instrument. Derivatives referencing game items may be regulated products in most jurisdictions; nothing here is legal advice.

1 · What this is

Three price indices over Counter-Strike 2 items, plus dated settlement fixings computed from them by fixed rules. Every input is a file committed to this repository by an automated collector (GitHub Actions, every 6 hours), so any counterparty can re-derive every published number bit-exactly from public data — the index operator has no discretion and nothing to assert that you cannot check.

Mark hygiene, all families: daily UTC buckets, median of intraday readings, a $0 price is never a mark (thin venues report zeros for no-sale windows), zero-volume days keep price but carry no volume, and per-item histories are append-only JSONL in data/history/.

3 · Settlement fixings

Averaged fixings are the anti-manipulation choice: to move an N-day mean by 1% an attacker must move every constituent ~1% for N days. Fixings publish with value null (“accruing”) until the minimum day count exists — a fixing is never backfilled or edited.

FixingDefinitionWindowMin daysDecimals
SETTLE-CASE-7DMean of daily Lab Case Index≤7 days32
SETTLE-CASE-30DMean of daily Lab Case Index≤30 days102
SETTLE-RATIO-30DMean of daily cash ratio (median third-party realized ÷ Steam price) ≤30 days74

Suitability, stated plainly: the case family has commodity-grade depth and is the only plausible candidate for continuous instruments; the cash ratio is a spread/convertibility mark suited to dated settlement only; the art index is never suitable for settlement (appraisal marks, single-sale jumps).

3a · Constituent changes — seasoning & the inclusion calendar

  • Founding cohort: items whose first recorded mark is on or before 2026-07-25 (the SMLX-2 adoption date) are included from their first day.
  • New listings season for 30 days before becoming eligible — new cases mechanically collapse 80–95% from launch-hype prices as drop supply floods in; that dynamic belongs to one item's supply schedule, not the market, and never enters the index.
  • Scheduled inclusion: an eligible item enters on the first day of the next calendar month after its seasoning completes. Because construction is chained, entry is return-neutral: the index level cannot move on inclusion day — there is no arithmetic to front-run, only the newcomer's future returns.
  • Both-days rule: an item contributes a return only when it has marks on both days of a pair and both days are on/after its inclusion date; art marks carry forward between sparse observations (appraisal semantics).
  • Liquids membership (≥5 median sold/day) is evaluated at computation time over trailing data; the published fixing records in data/settlements.jsonl are authoritative and never rewritten if a re-derivation with later data would classify differently.

4 · Corporate actions & patch events

  • Case leaves the active drop pool — no index action; the item keeps trading and its price keeps marking (this event is the market's core supply catalyst, not an anomaly).
  • Item delisted / renamed / made untradable by Valve — the constituent freezes at its last mark and is removed at the next methodology version; history is never rewritten.
  • Structural platform events (e.g., the July 2025 Trade Protection update) — fixings continue mechanically. Consumers of the fixings define their own halt/deferral rules; the methodology's only promise is that the published series is never edited after the fact.
  • Data-source loss (venue closes, blocks, or changes terms) — affected marks go null and carry forward per family rules; replacement sources require a version bump.

5 · Manipulation budget

A published floor estimate of the fee-burn required to move a fixing 1%: to shift one item's daily median an attacker must set the price on ≥50% of that day's prints (wash fraction 0.5), paying venue fees (Steam 15%, cash venues ~12%) on that volume; inventory and price risk are deliberately excluded, so true cost is higher. The index is an equal-weight geometric mean, so moving it requires moving every constituent; an N-day fixing multiplies the burn by N. 6 · Verification — check the operator

Every fixing publishes a SHA-256 hash over its canonical form ({methodology, name, window, days, values, value}). The button below re-derives the latest fixings in your browser from the committed series in data/index.json and compares hashes against data/settlement.json. From a shell:

node -e "const S=require('./settlement.js'),c=require('crypto');
const m=require('./data/index.json').market;
for(const [n,f] of Object.entries(S.computeAll(m.series)))
  console.log(n, c.createHash('sha256').update(S.canonical(f)).digest('hex'))"
